# LaPompe 0.1.0

The first LaPompe, for Firefox and Chrome, with TonTuyau 1.5.

- **Sends the YouTube cookie to TonTuyau** every N minutes (a setting: 30 by default, 5 at least) and on "Send the
  cookie now": the browser's `youtube.com` cookies as a Netscape cookie file, to TonTuyau's
  `PUT /api/extension/youtube-cookie`, with a browser-extension API key. The first send comes 30 seconds after the
  browser starts or the settings change.
- **Its popup**: which TonTuyau and its version, as whom (the key's name), the cookie as TonTuyau last answered (works,
  refused, not checked) or why it didn't go through (no YouTube cookie in this browser, TonTuyau unreachable, the key
  refused…), when it's sent next.
- **Its settings page**: TonTuyau's address, the API key, how often; Save and Test the connection ask the browser for
  leave to reach that address and youtube.com only.
- **"Add to TonTuyau"** next to Subscribe on a video's page, "Add channel to TonTuyau" on a channel's: disabled, saying
  why, until TonTuyau says 1.6 or later (then they call TonTuyau 1.6's endpoints, provisional until 1.6 defines them).
- English and French, as the browser; light and dark, as the browser.
