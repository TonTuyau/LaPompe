# LaPompe 0.3.0

- **Several TonTuyau**: LaPompe's settings take as many TonTuyau as you like, each with its address, the API key made in
  it, and a name of your own ("Home", "Lucas"; without one, its address shows). "Add a TonTuyau" adds one, "Remove"
  takes one away (and gives its address's permission back to the browser). Your TonTuyau of 0.2.0 is the first one:
  nothing to set up again.
- **Your YouTube cookie goes to every TonTuyau listed**, on the same alarm and with "Send the cookie now". The settings
  page says so: it's your signed-in YouTube session, add only TonTuyau whose owner you'd trust with it. A TonTuyau that
  can't be reached doesn't stop the others.
- **The popup shows each TonTuyau**: its name, address and version, whether YouTube took the cookie there, or why it
  didn't go through.
- **"Add to TonTuyau" with several TonTuyau**: the button sums up where the video or channel is ("In 1 of 2
  TonTuyau"), and opens a menu of your TonTuyau, each with where it stands there, and Add on those that don't have it.
  One that can't add (older than 2.0, or not tested yet) is greyed, saying why. With one TonTuyau, the button is as
  before: one click adds.
