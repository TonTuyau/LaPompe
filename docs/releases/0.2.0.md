# LaPompe 0.2.0

- **"Add to TonTuyau" works**, with TonTuyau 2.0 or later: on a YouTube video's page, one click and your TonTuyau
  downloads it; on a channel's, it subscribes to it (with its default tabs: videos, shorts and streams unless you change
  them on TonTuyau's YouTube page). It goes through TonTuyau's run queue.
- **The button says what your TonTuyau has**: "In TonTuyau" for a video it has or a channel it's subscribed to,
  "Waiting in TonTuyau" for one in its queue, instead of Add. For that, LaPompe tells your TonTuyau (only it) which
  YouTube video or channel page you open: the privacy policy and the stores' data declarations say so (Firefox:
  browsing activity, which it asks you to allow when it updates).
- LaPompe knows TonTuyau adds when TonTuyau says so (its connection test), not from its version: it asks again by
  itself when TonTuyau's version changes.
